# HEART STONE — approved appearance: SAVE & LOCK

Approved by John on October 24, 2026. Supersedes earlier heart-body/stick-figure and photorealistic appearance concepts.

## Identity
Adult feminine glamorous 2D cartoon woman, auburn waves, warm brown eyes, confident approachable expression, natural waist and moderately narrower hips as shown in the approved images. Consistent face and identity across clothing changes. Warm woman-to-woman confidante.

The HEART STONE heart-shaped compass must always be visibly worn somewhere on her: necklace by default; an approved brooch, bracelet or outfit detail can carry the same signature. It must not disappear when clothes change.

## Wardrobe and store requirements
Vary clothing while preserving identity and compass. Random rotation is a future feature and must respect context and user preferences. WORLD QUEST family mode requires family-appropriate outfits.

HEART STONE remains the model with her own cartoon face. With the adult user's explicit permission, a future fitting system can adapt her modeling proportions using that user's private sizes. Partner access to modeled previews requires that user's separate, revocable permission; authorization for HEART STONE to use sizes does not authorize disclosing sizes to a partner. Grants are per person and per relationship. Previews are appearance estimates, not guaranteed fit; purchases require separate approval.
